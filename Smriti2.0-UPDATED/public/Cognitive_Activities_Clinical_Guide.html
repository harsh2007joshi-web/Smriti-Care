SmritiCare NER: Cognitive Activities & Games Guide

Comprehensive Clinical & Functional Overview of Cognitive Wellness Exercises for Elderly Care

Target Group Elderly / Mild Cognitive Changes
Cultural Setting 8 North Eastern States (NER)
Adaptive Engine 3 Pacing Tiers (Gentle / Std / Active)
Clinical Intent Non-Diagnostic Wellness & Habit
🌿 Clinical Purpose & Design Philosophy

SmritiCare NER provides accessible, culturally familiar cognitive stimulation activities engineered to support memory preservation, executive functioning, multisensory processing, and daily routine retention. Unlike clinical screening tests that induce anxiety through timers and failure screens, every activity in SmritiCare is designed for dignity, positive reinforcement, and stress-free engagement.

🧭 1. The Foundation: Memory Baseline Check & Adaptive Pacing

Memory Baseline Check

Onboarding Calibration & Comfort Setting
Onboarding & Pace Calibration
🎯 Why It Is Used & What It Does

A quick, 2-minute 5-task calibration completed before regular games. Evaluates visual memory, sequence recall, pattern completion, and item matching to calculate an initial 0–100 score.

💡 How It Helps Patient & Caregiver

Assigns the patient into their ideal initial pacing tier: Gentle Support (0–59), Standard Rhythm (60–89), or Active Challenge (90–100). Eliminates frustration and prevents fatigue.

🎮 2. The 8 Targeted Cognitive Activities & Games

1. Memory Weave

Threads of My Life • Visual Association & Recall
Episodic & Semantic Memory
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Visual-spatial memory & associative pairing.
  • Short-term retention of hidden card locations.
  • Semantic recognition of North Eastern cultural artifacts.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 2 pairs (4 cards), 3 hints, 45s timer, full text hints.
  • Standard: 3 pairs (6 cards), 2 hints, 35s timer.
  • Active: 4 pairs (8 cards), 1 hint, 25s timer.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Stimulates the hippocampus and temporal lobe. Using culturally familiar symbols (Assam Tea, Jaapi, Muga Silk, Hornbill) taps into long-term semantic memory reserves (Reminiscence Therapy), making recall feel comforting rather than clinical.

2. Daily Routine

My Day Flow • Step Sequencing & Executive Function
Executive Function & Sequencing
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Executive sequencing and logical order planning.
  • Procedural memory retention (ADLs / Daily Living Activities).
  • Error detection and step reorganization.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 3 routine steps with supportive sequential hints.
  • Standard: 4 steps with balanced drag-and-drop ordering.
  • Active: 5 nuanced morning/evening steps.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Reinforces procedural memory pathways responsible for daily self-care (waking up, making tea, taking prescribed medicine, watering garden). Helps maintain independence and minimizes confusion regarding daily chronology.

3. Sensory Soundscape

Sounds of NER • Calming Auditory Recognition
Auditory Perception & Sensory Calming
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Auditory discrimination and sound-to-image association.
  • Cross-modal sensory processing (hearing & visual matching).
  • Sensory grounding and autonomic relaxation.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 2 options (1 distractor), 4.0s tone, unlimited replay.
  • Standard: 3 options, 3.2s tone, unlimited replay.
  • Active: 4 options (subtle audio differences), 2.8s tone.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Utilizes nature and environmental soundscapes (rainforest rain, Brahmaputra river gentle breeze, mountain birds, flute melody) to lower agitation (sundowning syndrome) while exercising auditory cortex processing.

4. Rhythm Weaver

Bihu & Folk Beats • Motor Timing & Working Memory
Motor Timing & Working Memory
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Auditory working memory and short-term sequence holding.
  • Fine motor coordination & rhythm synchronization.
  • Parietal-cerebellar motor planning.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 3-beat rhythm pattern, slow 800ms tempo, 600ms tolerance.
  • Standard: 4-beat rhythm pattern, 650ms tempo, 450ms tolerance.
  • Active: 5-beat rhythm pattern, 500ms tempo, 350ms tolerance.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Rhythm exercises stimulate auditory-motor neural synchronization. Folk beats (Bihu Dhol, Dholak, Peepa) evoke emotional joy, stimulate motor reflexes, and enhance finger agility on touch screens.

5. Dual-Task Journey

Focus & Stride • Divided Attention & Inhibitory Control
Divided Attention & Cognitive Control
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Divided attention (handling 2 simultaneous stimuli).
  • Reaction control and selective inhibition (Go / No-Go).
  • Working memory updating during ongoing motion.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 6 steps, 2 chimes, generous 3200ms response window.
  • Standard: 8 steps, 3 chimes, 2200ms response window.
  • Active: 10 steps, 4 chimes, 1400ms rapid response window.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Dual-tasking (e.g. stepping while responding to an auditory chime) directly simulates real-world challenges like walking while having a conversation. Proven in geriatric research to reduce fall risk and improve cognitive flexibility.

6. Living Market

Bazaar & Daily Essentials • Semantic Memory & Budgeting
Executive Function & Working Memory
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Item list retention and working memory manipulation.
  • Category discrimination (vegetables, tea, spices, staples).
  • Basic mental arithmetic and budget selection.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 2 shopping list items, 2 market distractors.
  • Standard: 3 shopping items, 3 market distractors.
  • Active: 4 shopping items, 4 market distractors with price balance.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Simulates an everyday bazaar visit (Ima Keithel / Guwahati Market). Preserves community interaction confidence, category identification, and practical problem-solving skills required for daily living.

7. Landmark Pathfinder

Heritage Trails of NE India • Spatial Navigation
Spatial Orientation & Topographical Memory
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Spatial navigation, orientation, and mental mapping.
  • Topographical memory (recognizing spatial waypoints).
  • Planning multi-step directional movements.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 2 landmarks on a 3x3 grid with direct path visual guide.
  • Standard: 3 landmarks on a 4x4 grid with step counter.
  • Active: 4 landmarks across a complex 4x4 path network.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Navigating famous NE landmarks (Kamakhya Temple, Loktak Lake, Majuli Island, Tawang Monastery) maintains mental spatial maps in the parietal cortex, countering spatial disorientation and wandering tendencies.

8. Cognitive Mini-Test

Gentle Orientation & Recall Reflection
Temporal Orientation & Immediate Recall
🧠 Targeted Cognitive Domain
  • Temporal orientation (current day of week, current month).
  • Immediate verbal and visual stimulus recall.
  • Everyday wellness awareness and reflection.
🕹️ Gameplay & Adaptive Mechanics
  • Gentle: 3 simple questions with 2 large multiple-choice options.
  • Standard: 4 questions with 3 choices.
  • Active: 5 questions covering orientation, recall, and wellness habits.
🌟 Therapeutic Benefit to the Patient Provides a calm, daily orientation reality check without clinical intimidation. Helps elderly patients stay anchored to time, date, season, and positive daily wellness routines.

9. Memory Garden

Blossom Wellness & Emotional Positive Reinforcement
Emotional Well-Being & Habit Formation
🌸 Gamification & Persistence Mechanic

Every completed activity generates soothing "Memory Dew Drops". Patients water virtual indigenous blossoms (Rhodo, Lotus, Wild Orchid, Blue Vanda) in their personalized 3D/2D garden.

💖 Dopaminergic Positive Reinforcement

Replaces stressful "Game Over" and numerical scores with visual growth of peaceful flowers. Builds intrinsic motivation and supports a daily cognitive exercise routine.

🤝 4. Clinical & Practical Value for Caregivers & Healthcare
👨‍👩‍👧 For Families & Caregivers
  • Automated Activity Tracking: View daily completions, active streaks, and engagement history in real time.
  • Objective Trend Monitoring: Tracks longitudinal accuracy and reaction comfort across 5 cognitive dimensions.
  • Reduced Caregiver Stress: Guided audio and adaptive pacing allow patients to enjoy activities independently.
🏥 For Healthcare Systems & MDoNER / MoHFW
  • Scalable Tele-Rehabilitation: Deploys culturally attuned cognitive stimulation to remote NER hill districts.
  • No Special Hardware Needed: Works seamlessly on any Android smartphone, tablet, or web browser.
  • Early Subtle Decline Detection: Multi-session rolling performance metrics provide non-invasive longitudinal wellness telemetry.
⚠️ Non-Diagnostic Clinical Safety Notice SmritiCare NER activities and baseline check are intended exclusively for cognitive wellness stimulation, daily routine maintenance, and non-diagnostic progress tracking. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, or cure dementia, Alzheimer's disease, or any medical neurological condition.
